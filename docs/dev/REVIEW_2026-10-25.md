## Findings, most serious first

### 1. Blocks the final run: the decay ladder fails to build at allowed points (dp_medium.Ladder, ValueError)

Where: src/dp_medium.py:59 (NEXP = 24), :637-639 (the cut-bin count is checked against NEXP), :849-852 (the
ValueError). It is raised from dark_portal_solver.py:501 (the cosmological ladder) and :1336 (the Galactic ladder).

What happens: a rung's plan may hold at most 24 cut bins per channel. Close to a channel's threshold, the window can
be wide in z while k_T^2 stays tiny across it. The conservative test for "wholly inside the window" then fails for
every bin, so each bin becomes a cut bin. More than 24 such bins and the whole ladder is refused. The run raises, and
so does the fit at that point.

The case I took apart (le_ltau_ul, M = 0.0316 eV, g = 1e-5, m_l = 1e-3; Galactic ladder, rung sigma = 6.5e-4 eV^2):
- The channel is the flip nu(state 1) -> nubar(state 0).
- Its window is z = 0.067-0.107, with k_T^2 <= 0.003 z M^2 inside it.
- That spans the offsets k = 110..135: 26 cut bins.
- The channel's rate is negligible, but the ladder fails anyway.
- With NEXP raised to 512 in a copy of the module, this ladder builds. The largest count is 26, at this one rung.

How often it happens: I built the ladders the way production does, through the solver with a hook that stops right
after the ladder, at (M, g, m_l) grids of the ultralight planes (script review/ladder_scan.py):
- Coarse grid: 5 planes, M = 1e-24 to 0.03 eV every 1.5 decades, g every decade, m_l = 1e-6, 1e-3, 0.01, 0.1.
  - Cosmological ladders: 736 built, none failed.
  - Galactic ladders: 2742 built, 1 failed (the case above).
- Fine grid: le_lmu_ul and le_ltau_ul, M = 1e-4 to 0.063 eV, g = 1e-9 to 1e-2.
  - Cosmological: 17 failures, all at 'combined' points (g >= 1e-4), m_l = 1e-6, M = 6e-3 to 1.6e-2 eV.
  - Galactic: 37 failures, g >= 3e-7, every cosmology mode.
- Dense grid in M and m_l: M = 2.5e-3 to 0.063 eV in steps of 0.05 decade, 11 values of m_l, g = 1e-5 and 1e-3.
  - Galactic: 188 of 1072 ladders failed, all at allowed points.
  - The failures fill M = 6e-3 to 4.5e-2 eV for m_l <= 3e-3 eV: 12-16 of the 52 ladders per m_l value fail.
  - None failed for m_l >= 0.01 eV.
  - Cosmological: 64 of 536 'combined' ladders (g = 1e-3) failed, over the same M and m_l band. None of the 466
    'dark_portal' ladders at g = 1e-5 failed: their sigma range does not reach the failing rung.
  - Every failure is code 1 (NEXP).

The plane raster has a column at M = 0.01 eV, inside this band, and the refinements add more. The fits also start
the lightest-mass profile at 1e-6 eV. So the final run would crash at a set of allowed points of both lepton-number
planes.

To reproduce: build the solver parameters at le_ltau_ul (m_Zd_eV 0.0316, g_D 1e-5, m_lightest_eV 1e-3) and call
dark_portal_solver.galactic_allsky. It raises "dp_medium.Ladder: the plan of rung 1239 (sigma 6.516e-04) failed,
code 1". review/fail_point.py does this and saves the ladder's inputs.

Fix:
- Take the capacity from the array (plan_k.shape[2]) instead of the global NEXP, and on err == 1 rebuild with twice
  the capacity (the same for NRUN). The memory is nr x 36 x NEXP x 16 bytes, about 25 MB at NEXP = 64.
- Better still, give single-rung ranges an exact "inside for every parent position" test, so that marginal bins
  become one-rung runs.
- Add the ladder scan as a pre-flight check of the final run's grid: every column's M at m_l from 1e-6 to 0.1 eV,
  both ladders.

### 2. Should fix: the validity measure zeta is never recorded, and the limits may sit where it is not small

Where: dp_medium._psi (:735-750) computes zeta per rung, and step_full updates `ladder.diag[0]`. Nothing ever reads
`Ladder.diag`: dark_portal_solver.py:784 passes it in and drops it, and the Galactic sweep never updates it. The notes
(line 397, "recorded in every run") and SIMPLIFICATIONS (e)(ii) say it is recorded.

Measured (the ladder scans above; the maximum over the rungs a run uses, at the allowed points):
- zeta_max is about 1.2e-4 (g/M)^2 for m_l <= 0.01 eV (le_lmu and le_ltau alike).
- It is about 1.8e-3 (g/M)^2 at m_l = 0.1 eV. The pure-vacuum value is lower (2.8e-5 (g/M)^2): the maximum comes from
  the potential's range.
- Hence 0.12-0.15 at g/M = 31.6 eV^-1 (m_l <= 0.01) and 0.18 at g/M = 10 (m_l = 0.1). Just below the unitarity
  line at m_l = 0.1 (g/M of about 17) it is about 0.5.
- The design doc says zeta exceeds 0.1 "only for g/M >~ 30 eV^-1 in vacuum". At m_l = 0.1 eV that point is
  g/M = 7.5 eV^-1.

Why it matters: the earlier campaign's le_lmu_ul boundary sat at g/M of about 100 eV^-1 from M = 1e-27 to 1e-6 eV
(output/analysis/campaign_ml/now_ic26/le_lmu_ul, old decay model, void as a result). The new model drains less: the
doc's points keep 0.4-0.95 of the flux at g/M <= 10. So the new boundary will plausibly lie at g/M of 10-100, where
zeta is 0.01 to above 1. There the eigenstate picture (H - i Gamma / 2 treated as diagonal) carries corrections of
order zeta.

Fix:
- Return the largest zeta of each run (cosmological and Galactic) and store it in the fit records.
- Overlay zeta = 0.05 and 0.1 on the final exclusion plots.
- Where the 95% contour runs through zeta > 0.05, either shade it as "model approximation fails" (like the unitarity
  region) or check a few points with the non-Hermitian H - i Gamma / 2.

### 3. Should fix (or quantify before the final run): the extragalactic beam's decays while it crosses the Galaxy

The beam's decays during the Galaxy crossing are not modelled; the transit is coherent and decay-free. This is
documented in DECAY_IN_MEDIUM section "Decays where the transport has none", notes lines 446-450 and 2746-2749.

The doc's own estimate: the lowest states decay there by more than 1e-3 for g/M >= 0.1 eV^-1 with
sigma/dm21^2 >= 3.7. Given finding 2, that is probably exactly where the limits will lie. The doc calls it "the one
place they could change a result".

Fix: before the final run, measure the beam's survival across the Galaxy with dp_medium.decay_sweep along a few lines
of sight, at points on the expected boundary. If it is above 1e-3, model it (the Galactic component already does it
per line of sight).

### 4. Should fix: the Galactic sweep still alternates the jumps and the decays

Where: src/dp_galactic_path.py:83-96 (Strang alternation, CYCLE_STEP = 0.25, CYCLE_MAX = 64).

The convergence audit found this alternation first order where a decay is stiff within a sub-step (its R3: the drain
rate 13% low at CYCLE_STEP 0.25). Beyond CYCLE_MAX the sub-steps hold more than 0.25 events. The cosmology now solves
each bin exactly (dp_sweep.step_full); the Galaxy does not. dp_sweep's docstring (lines 2-5) says it serves "the
Galactic paths' segments"; it does not.

Measured so far:
- Before the in-medium change: 0.6-3% where the processes drain the Galactic flux (notes line 2614).
- With it: the cycling point le_lmu_ul (1e-8, 1e-8) is "pending" in the design doc's Galactic table. The potential
  points agree to 1e-8.

Fix: call step_full per segment (no injection or absorption, z = 0, the segment's length as the time). Or at least run
n_split = 4 against 1 at the cycling points before the final run.

### 5. Should fix: the Galactic part is not extended above the grid's top at points routed to 'si'

Where: dark_portal_solver._grid_extension (:1392-1413) returns k = 0 whenever `_cosmology_mode(params, E) == 'si'`.

The Galactic sweep still runs its processes there: the jumps, the in-medium decays and the loss. The scans found
Galactic ladders with a nonzero potential at le points whose cosmology is 'si'. Elsewhere the Galactic part is
extended only to the template's end, 1e8 GeV, about 0.52 decade at a 3e16 eV top. validate_transport's check H covers
the extragalactic flux only. So what comes down from above the top into the Galactic component is checked nowhere.

Fix: decide the Galactic extension by whether the Galactic processes act (below 1 keV, with the processes on),
whatever the cosmology mode. Add a Galactic grid-top check: the same run with a template-limited top half a decade
higher.

### 6. Should fix: the in-medium decays have no absolute check on production configurations

tools/validate_transport.py check F (the decay against the exact nu_3 survival) is now restricted to the dp_ planes.
Those route to dp_si wherever the decays act (the scans: every dp_*_ul point with decays is 'si'). So dp_medium is
checked on production configurations only by self-convergence (steps, grid), never against an absolute answer.

Fix: add two cheap absolute checks to validate_transport on the le planes:
- The number: the run with decays only (jumps and loss off) keeps the number, apart from what leaves the grid's
  bottom. That is computable from Ladder plan_tot minus what is deposited.
- The top bin's survival: the heaviest eigenstate at the top bin, with no inflow from above, survives as
  exp(-int Gamma_a(sigma(z)) dt). Integrate Gamma_a independently, as test_medium._exact_widths does.

### 7. Should fix: decision A's lightest-mass cap can now switch itself off silently

Where: analysis/exact_profile.py:538-545.

lightest_cap now returns None (no cap) when the configuration's path does not exist, or when not_allowed_boundary
raises ValueError or KeyError. Before, these raised. In production the path is absolute (config.load), so this should
not trigger. But if it does (a moved or packed configuration, a broken boundary computation), the fits silently go
beyond the unitarity cap, with no trace in the records.

Fix: record the reason in the fit record and warn. Or raise for the planes whose boundary must exist (le_*_ul,
dp_*_ul).

### 8. Should fix (small): a seam between the points with and without decays

Where: dark_portal_solver.py:418-429 and :568. The exact injection integral (inj_I) is used only where the decays
act. Elsewhere below 1 keV the injection is taken at each step's low-redshift end, and the step count is 200 instead
of 400.

With calc_R_source, the end-point injection is high by 0.41% (p = 2), 0.67% (p = 2.735) and 0.78% (p = 3) at 200
steps, and by half that at 400. So the flux steps by about 0.7%, slightly p-dependent, where _decay_acts switches.
norm_astro absorbs most of it.

Fix: use the exact injection for every dark-portal-phase run below 1 keV. The MeV planes stay untouched, since the
switch can stay at m_Zd < 1 keV.

### 9. Minor: the condition [G, S] = 0 is not needed, and the notes' reason for it is wrong

I computed the amplitude by brute force: explicit light-front spinors for every mass component, the mediator's
polarization sum -J.J* + |q.J|^2/M^2 with the in-medium q, and the coherent sum over the eigenvectors (script
review/bf_amp.py). It reproduces the coded kept and flipped |A|^2 to 1e-11, for both kinds, with Majorana phases,
three textures, vacuum and potential.

It also does so for a coupling that does not commute with the texture, G = [[1, .5, 0], [.5, -1, 0], [0, 0, 0]]
against S = diag(1, -1, 0). The formula, written in k_T, is exact for any S. The vacuum energy mismatch the notes
single out (line 2650-2654, "sums to sigma <b|[G', S']|a>, which is zero") cancels against the mass terms
identically.

Fix: correct the notes' sentence and the docstring (dp_medium.py:14-17). The ValueError at :767-775 can stay as a
guard, but it is not a physical requirement.

## What I checked and found right

- **The MeV and secret-interaction planes are unchanged.** I ran tools/compare_code_versions.py on Tycho: plan, then
  run with 32 workers for the old clone and the new one, then compare. It covers 952 units: the 5 MeV planes and 19
  SI planes, 40 points each, with random lightest masses.
  - SI: F and MF bit for bit, every unit.
  - MeV: F, G, MF and MG differ by at most 1.1e-14 relative, often bit for bit.
  - That 1e-14 is the old code's own run-to-run scatter. Two runs of a0e876d against each other differ by the same
    1.1e-14, with even fewer bit-for-bit units: the in-memory thermal caches depend on which units a worker ran
    first. So there is no change attributable to the edits.
- **The amplitude, both kinds, the flips.** The spinor brute force agrees to 1e-11 (finding 9). The COEF and
  _ycoeffs polynomials equal the formula to 3e-12 (2000 random points).
- **The flip nu_i -> nubar_i is physical.** It is the medium-induced, Majoron-like decay through the longitudinal
  mode, with Goldstone coupling 2 m_i G'_ii / M. That needs Majorana masses that break the U(1): the same
  assumption as dp_decay's vacuum rates and decision A.
  - It opens only for the kind whose energy the potential raises.
  - Its narrow-window rate in the plans matches G'^2 sigma^3 S'^3 / (3 pi M^2 m^2) to 1e-5 (m_l = 0.01 and 0.1; review/flip_check.py).
- **The Weyl closure** (a kept a -> b needs mu_a > mu_b + M^2) is right, and the skips in _plans are exact for both
  rels.
- **Light-front kinematics:** the window k_T^2 = (1-z)(z mu_a - mu'_b(z sigma)) - z M^2 and the root-product trick in
  _window_y are right.
- **The kinds' conventions:** c = U^dag W, nubar's c = U^T W-bar, G_s = G'*, D^2 - sigma S'*.
- **step_full's bookkeeping:**
  - Densities per unit energy against numbers; dt, jump_I and decay_I.
  - Lam is per dt, and Lam / (dsc zp1) is the physical width.
  - phi1 and phi2 give the exact time average for constant sources.
  - fcap keeps the number (DECAY_STEP_CAP = 1e9, RATIO 1e7: its error is <= 1e-7).
  - The own-bin flips go into the other kind.
- **Supporting pieces:**
  - The rung bookkeeping between a parent's plan and its daughters' psi.
  - deposit and gains clearing their buffers.
  - The vacuum rungs (bin p at rung p + 2).
  - The decay_step refactor (the same as before for mass states).
  - window_plan's cloud-in-cell bounds and dp_si's <1/u>.
- **Routing:**
  - _cosmology_mode and _decay_acts agree with propagate_spectrum's criterion.
  - _grid_extension is decided on the given grid, and the mode does not change on the extended one.
  - DECAY_Z_REFINE: nothing reads params['n_z_bins'] after _flux_setup. The thermal caches key on N_z, N_E and the
    grid's ends. The disk flux cache keys on a digest of every src/*.py (new modules included).
  - The analysis's upper spectral window [E_break, inf) does inject into the extension.
- **Tests on Tycho:** tests/test_medium.py, test_dp_sweep.py, test_decay.py, test_galactic_path.py,
  test_relic_scattering.py and test_event_likelihood.py, slow tests included: 56 passed in 45 s (job 66913981, 32
  workers). The suite as it stands includes DECAY_Z_REFINE's test.
- **Ladder cost:** 0.06-0.5 s per ladder once compiled (the scans).
